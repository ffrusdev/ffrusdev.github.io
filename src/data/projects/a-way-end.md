## The pitch
Somewhere deep in the dungeon lies a legendary sword. To reach it, the player has to fight through a series of hand-built levels, and every level stays sealed until they find its **lost relic**. Monsters guard the way, and potions are what keep them going.

Classic dungeon crawling built around one clear loop: *explore, survive, find the relic, go deeper.*

## My role
**Solo developer, built in about two months.** Every part of A Way End was made by me:

- **Game design & level design:** the concept and core loop, plus every level built by hand: where the monsters, potions and relics go, and the route to the next floor.
- **Programming:** all gameplay systems in **Unity 6**, including the **inventory system** for picking up, holding and using items like potions and weapons.
- **Pixel art & animation:** every sprite drawn and animated in **Aseprite**: the hero's walk, idle and death animations, the dungeon and grassland tilesets, and the props and items (chests, potions, weapons, torches, the sword in the stone).
