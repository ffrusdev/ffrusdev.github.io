## The pitch
Somewhere deep in the dungeon lies a legendary sword. The player goes in to claim it, and the dungeon fights back: monsters stand between them and the blade, and lost relics wait to be uncovered along the way.

One clear goal, and everything the dungeon throws in the way. A small, focused 2D adventure that's easy to pick up: *find the sword, survive the dungeon.*

## My role
**Solo developer.** Every part of A Way End was made by me:

- **Game design:** the concept, the dungeon's layout and flow, and how monsters and relics are placed along the way to the sword.
- **Programming:** all gameplay systems, built in **Unity 6**.
- **Pixel art & animation:** every sprite drawn and animated in **Aseprite**: the hero's walk, idle and death animations, the dungeon and grassland tilesets, and the props and items (chests, potions, weapons, torches, the sword in the stone).
