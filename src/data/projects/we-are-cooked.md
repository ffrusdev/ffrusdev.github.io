## The pitch
Captured by the wicked **Wizard King**, two teams of chefs have to cook their way to freedom. Every order is a real-world dish with a fantasy twist, made from ingredients that are literally out of this world. Keep your kitchen under control, or turn the chaos against the other team. Cook well, or you're **cooked**.

## Features
- **2 vs 2 cook-off.** Team up with a friend and out-cook the rival pair before the clock runs out.
- **Real dishes, fantasy ingredients.** Familiar recipes, built from magical ingredients.
- **Chaos as a weapon.** Steal from the other team when they're not looking (X on keyboard, Circle on a PS4 controller).
- **Online lobbies.** Create or join a lobby with a code, ready up and start cooking.
- **Playful, colourful look.** Chunky characters, cosy stone kitchens and big readable UI.
- Inspired by *Overcooked*.

## My role
**Main programmer** on a team of four, working with our director, main artist and main 3D modeller. It started as a university project, built from Code Monkey's base game template, and we're still developing it.

## What's next
The itch.io build is an early demo. Next on our list: cleaner collisions, character select in every mode, finishing levels 2 and 3, a proper tutorial and win screen, and a warning when your connection is poor.
