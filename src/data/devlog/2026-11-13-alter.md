New shelf on CH01: **TOOLS**. First on it is **Alter**, a small Windows app I made because I wanted a second Claude desktop signed in to another account.

Alter runs separate copies of any app. Each copy has its own sign-in, settings, icon and taskbar button, so a work Chrome and a personal Chrome can sit side by side. It runs on the PowerShell that comes with Windows, so there's nothing to install. [Read about it and download it](/projects/alter/).
