CH03 is now a shelf of short films. New on it: **Hammer of Gods**, my take on how Mjolnir was made. Two dwarven brothers at the forge, and Loki knocking at the door at the worst possible moment. Made solo in about six weeks with Blender and Unreal Engine 5. You can spin the smithy and one of the forest trees in 3D. [Step into the forge](/models/hammer-of-gods/).

Xephos got 8 new stills as well, from the crew quarters out to the asteroid rings.
